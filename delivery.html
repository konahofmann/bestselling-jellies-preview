Delivery and ordering

From the UK, worldwide and overseas

Orders are arranged by message on Instagram. You get the price, the postage to your country and a confirmation that your friend is still available, all in one conversation.

Ordering

Message, confirm, done

Pick a plush from the collection or the feed and send a message to @bestselling_jellies. Prices are not listed publicly and are shared on request.

Once card payment links are set up, a button on each product will take you straight to a secure checkout. Until then, everything is arranged by message.

Delivery

Sent from the United Kingdom

Anna Plushies delivers worldwide and overseas. Postage depends on where you are, so ask for the cost to your country in your message.

Good to know

Ask first, then order.

Pieces on the feed can sell out quickly. A short message is the fastest way to know what is still there.

Questions

Before you order

How much does a plush cost?

Prices are shared on request. Send a message on Instagram with the name or a screenshot of the plush you like.

Do you ship to my country?

Delivery is worldwide and overseas, sent from the United Kingdom. Ask for the postage to your country when you message.

How do I pay?

Payment is arranged in the conversation on Instagram. Card payment links on this site are being prepared.

Is my favourite still available?

The feed changes quickly. A message is the only way to be sure.
